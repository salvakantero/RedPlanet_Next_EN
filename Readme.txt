
===========================
RED PLANET NEXT v1.0 (2026)
===========================
   for ZX Spectrum NEXT

Lead our hero into the underground of the old space station to blow up the entire complex and wipe out its evil inhabitants.
But beware, the environment is dangerous. Avoid coming into contact with the aliens, and falling into the lava or radioactive waste pits.


ITEMS
=====

EXPLOSIVES: To successfully complete the mission you must collect the 10 explosives scattered around the station and place them in the explosives depot, deep inside the Martian base.
Once placed, you must return to the control centre and activate the detonator.

KEY CARD: You will sometimes need to access restricted areas locked behind access cards, so you must locate these cards before continuing your mission.

GATES: You can only open them if you carry a card.
The cards are single-use, but the gate will remain open once unlocked.

AMMO: You can fire plasma bullets at your enemies, but be careful not to waste ammunition, as it is scarce.
Reloads are scattered throughout the complex.

OXYGEN: Keep an eye on your oxygen cylinders and find new ones before they run out, or you will lose a life instantly.

BURGER, CAKE, DONUT: Sí, a los marcianos también les gustan. 
Cómetelos si quieres ver aumentar tu puntuación.


ENEMIES
=======

INFECTED: Former comrades who attempted the mission and did not make it back.
The bite of the aracnoviruses keeps them in an undead state.
They move in straight lines and can be very fast; take them out before they infect you.

ARACNOVIRUS/PELUSOID: Erratic and fast moving.
They usually travel through the air. They are the most numerous aliens that have colonised the base.

PELUSOID FANTY: They are very intelligent. They can lie in wait for your arrival.
If they see or hear you, they will pounce. In that case, finish them off as quickly as possible.

MAIN MENU
=========

This consists of a single screen with the game options and a short summary.

From the main menu you can:
- Start a new game with keyboard (press 1)
- Start a new game with joystick (press 2)
- Redefine keys. The new configuration will be saved (press 3)


GAME CONTROL (default)
======================

jump:       Q or joystick up
left:       O or joystick left
right:      P or joystick right
fire:       SPACE or joystick button
pause:      H (on/off)
music:      M (on/off)
